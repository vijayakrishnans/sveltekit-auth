## Secrets (names only)

| secret | where | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` | the workspace | BROKERED from its Cloudflare connection (`workers-deploy`): deploys the Worker and its static assets, and sets its secrets |
| `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` | the workspace | the connection's account id — a fact, not a credential |
| `AUTH_SECRET` | each Worker (`sveltekit-auth-web-stage`, `sveltekit-auth-web-prod`) | seals Auth.js's session cookies; generated on the Worker by its first deploy and kept by every later one. A workspace secret of the same name (`secret://…/sveltekit-auth/<env>/AUTH_SECRET`) replaces it on the next deploy |
| `AUTH_<PROVIDER>_ID`, `AUTH_<PROVIDER>_SECRET` | the workspace, optional | an OAuth app's keys (`secret://…/sveltekit-auth/<env>/AUTH_GITHUB_ID` and so on); each deploy pushes the ones that are set to its Worker, and that provider's sign-in appears |

Brokered secrets are minted per run, so no long-lived Cloudflare credential
is at rest. AUTH_SECRET is never in the repository, a log or this file. What the workspace holds right now (every
secret by name, and its integrations and their health) is on the console's
Secrets and Integrations pages for `org_74726a62159c4909af3be42262eaf6ed`.
